Cloud Guard

Finds the AWS spend you forgot about

  • Stale snapshots, gp2 volumes that should be gp3

How GuardInfra works

🔑

Read-only, always

Cloud Guard assumes a role in your account through STS. It can look, it cannot change anything, and no long-lived credentials are ever stored.

🧾

Every number is traceable

A finding names the resource, the observation behind it, and where the price came from. If you cannot verify a figure in your own console, it should not be here.

⚠️

Confidence, not certainty

An unattached volume is provable. A 90-day-old snapshot might back an AMI. Findings say which is which, so you know what to act on and what to check first.

🚧

Shipped means shipped

Live means running right now. Coming soon means there is nothing to try yet. No product here is marked available before it works.
