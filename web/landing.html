How It Works

1

Deploy

Launch our CloudFormation template, then paste the role ARN back here. Takes about two minutes. Creates a read-only IAM role assumed via STS — we never hold long-term credentials.

2

Scan

Scans EC2, EBS, Elastic IPs, snapshots, RDS, S3 and Cost Explorer across every region you have enabled.

3

Act

Prioritized findings with risk levels, estimated savings, and Slack alerts. Fix issues before they become incidents.
